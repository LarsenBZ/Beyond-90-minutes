About This Project

Why I build this

I have been a Football (Soccer) fan since I was 5 years old. I remember matches from 2013 Clásicos between Real Madrid and Barcelona, admiring the quality of players such as Cristiano Ronaldo, Bale, Xabi Alonso and more. When I was 13, I wanted to become a sports journalist. Discussing and analyzing games, the transfer market, and being part of the spectacle that Football brings. That's not the direction I have decided to take in the end for college, but I still want my passion for Football to persist and go beyond what a Football match is. It's not just the score after 90 minutes. There is much more than that in every game. Writing the articles even though must likely no one will read them but myself helps me think back on my childhood and the present showing through words a very special part of my life.

This is why I decided to start the project of Beyond 90 Minutes. It has turned into my personal diary and a place where i can break down matches, improve my writing, and enjoy Football. Even though I want to pursue an education in Nursing, I will keep the young version of me through the website.

How I built it

I am not a coding person and still lack a lot of knowledge. The website's structure was built with the help of AI assistant Claude which was very helpful when trying to have my ideas come to life. I had to test, ask questions, and rebuilt until it worked the way it was intended. I used a couple of tools such as VS Code, GitHub repositories, and Cloudflare for the API's used in the website. Before AI I didn't know about free API's and how they worked, but they were extremely helpful in the stats part of the website. Football data such as standings, schedules, live scores all update automatically with public sports API's. The articles and synopsis for each Real Madrid match are all from the knowledge I have gathered throughout the years and the daily news and changes that happen in Football.
