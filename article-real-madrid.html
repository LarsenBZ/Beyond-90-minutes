Real Madrid Have Replaced Kroos and Modrić. But Have They Replaced What They Provided?

By Braulio Zelaya · August 2026 · 6 min read

Since 2014/15, Kroos and Modrić became the core of Real Madrid's midfield dominance. Tactically, the Real Madrid board faces a new challenge in replacing their unique ability to dictate tempo and control possession against low-block defenses.

"Replacing output is easy. Replacing rhythm is the hard part."

Analyzing the Lineup Structure

Under current managerial setups, Real Madrid shifts between a 4-2-3-1 and 4-3-3 shape. While squad depth in attack remains high, controlling transitions in high-stakes Champions League fixtures requires precise midfield distribution.
