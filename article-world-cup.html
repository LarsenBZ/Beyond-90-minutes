Is the World Cup a EURO + Brazil and Argentina?

By Braulio Zelaya · August 2026 · 4 min read

Although the caption may even discredit the World Cup it is a discussion that occurs more and more. The World Cup as its name indicates it is meant to show which country has the best group of players in a knockout tournament. However not every country has an equal chance to make it far. Historically outside of UEFA and Conmebol elite teams have fewer success in the later stages of the tournament.

Also there are only 8 distinct World Cup winners: Brazil (5), Germany (4), Italy (4), Argentina (3), Uruguay (2), France (2), Spain (2), England (1). This list makes an emphasis that only European teams and South American giants can compete, particularly Brazil and Argentina.

"The European Champions are the elite of football but, the knockout stage creates historical matches that create a chance for any nation to create history."

Is this 100% of the time?

NO. The World Cup although dominated by European countries in the final stages of the tournament. There are always surprises such as Colombia 2014, Costa Rica 2014, Morocco 2022, and Cape Verde 2026. These teams demonstrate that other smaller nations can compete. However, at the same rate as there are surprises outside of European nations there are also smaller European nations which get far in the tournament. Examples are Belgium 2018, Croatia 2018/2022, Norway 2026. These teams although they don't have a World Cup they showed competition towards the giants. Also there are cases in which teams can compete against big teams in isolated matches. What I mean by this is for example Paraguay 2026 which although they lost 1-4 against the United States in the group stages, they were able to still qualify to the round of 32 and beat Germany. Germany with stars such as Florian Wirtz, Jamal Musiala, Joshua Kimmich, Manuel Neuer and more. Some may be against the tactics of Paraguay as they showed an extremely defensive tactic to go to penalties and win. These teams aren't going to play the way the rivals want them to play, they make great use of their South American mindset of winning no matter how the opponent plays. Even if Paraguay didn't play the "best or beautiful" football they were able to compete. Which is how the rest of teams look forwards to competing in the way that suits their players best. So yes the smaller teams from other regions besides Europe + Argentina and Brazil can compete.

Why are European teams so dominant?

Many may argue that it's due to having more countries in the World Cup meaning more chances of a good performing team. However, I'd argue that the amount of teams or the population of a country do not significantly impact a country to do well or not at the World Cup. For example in the recent World Cup Cape Verde with a population of 530,000 was able to compete against Argentina the finalist with a population of 46.0 million. There is more to football than the luck of having better players. A group of players isn't a team, which can be demonstrated by Germany in since 2018 which doesn't show a clear connection with the manager failing to have a performance of a 4 time World Cup Champion. Every match can be won by any team at the World Cup. Of course the favorites are the favorites and will most likely to be able to reach a quarterfinal than the rest of teams but it's not impossible.

Why was the quarterfinal of the World Cup formed by 6 European teams?

  • European teams are formed by mostly players which play in the top European leagues which are largely considered the best in the world. This emphasizes that they are already used to matches of high quality.
  • They have higher chances of individualistic performances that win games. It's more likely for Mbappe, Olise and Dembele to have a performance that wins the game than the front 3 of nations that don't have players of this caliber.
  • They play in better infrastructures for Football. Many countries don't have the same Football culture or have investment on the sport compared to European teams. This means that players have better development in Europe or in countries such as Brazil and Argentina were talent is highly valued.

Does the World Cup format favors the top teams?

No, I would argue that the knockout stage favors a smaller team on avarage. For example in a "league style format" Paraguay would likely not finish higher than Germany because Germany is more likely to be more consistent based on their players. However, in a knockout stage anything can happen and Paraguay can win against Germany and do a better World Cup even if "they aren't the better team".

So is the World Cup really a EURO with Argentina and Brazil?

Yes and No. Yes there will likely still be European dominance in the World Cup if the trends continue with a combination of 2 surprise teams or Argentina and Brazil in the quarterfinals and onwards. However, there are always teams that gain the love of Football fans such as in this past World Cup Cape Verde that demonstrate that the World Cup is more than who finishes 1st. These surprise teams make the World Cup as special as the historical World Cup Champions. The European Champions are the elite of football but, the knockout stage creates historical matches that create a chance for any nation to create history.
