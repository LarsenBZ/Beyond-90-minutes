Has VAR Made Football Worse?

By Braulio Zelaya · August 2026 · 4 min read

There is a Football before and after refereeing with the assistance of VAR. VAR is a tool that assists the referee in certain situations that allow the referee to review a play if the VAR referees think that the referee on the pitch should review their decision.

When is VAR used?

VAR is used:

  1. In goals and their build up checking whether the offensive side committed a foul, offside, or a handball leading to the goal.
  2. During penalty decisions the referee has to take a decision in the moment. If the VAR referees see something that the referee did not see that could change the pitch referee's decision then VAR will call the referee to the review area. Then the referee will decide if his decision will stand or not.
  3. In direct red cards VAR is able to call the referee for review if they spot something that could change the referee's decision.
  4. Mistaken identity are cases in which the referee penalizes the wrong player. This is looked at by VAR and corrected.

How can a tool to make football more "fair" potentially make football worse?

Most football fans will likely agree that VAR is more "fair", but it can remove the emotion out of the game. Before VAR even if the goal was offside or not if the linesman didn't raise their flag then fans could celebrate with all emotion. Now if in the play there seems to be even the chance of an offside it's difficult to celebrate. Many times the situation involves goal -> celebration -> wait -> VAR lines -> correct frame of offside -> decision -> celebrate again. The between the goal and final decision can be fast but also really slow especially in smaller leagues. The VAR offside lines specifically can have errors too. Even with the semi-automatic system a person has to choose the correct frame from when the player passes the ball which can lead to mistakes. VAR disrupts the flow of the game many times killing the tension or emotion from the game. Also in other instances such as handballs and penalties fans still argue about the referee's decision as it is still within the referee's criteria for what is the correct decision.

"VAR is killing the raw emotion of the game."
— Andros Townsend, to the BBC
"You must be mentally strong when VAR is not on our side."
— Pep Guardiola, via the Sunday Mirror

Moments that would have changed Football with VAR

With VAR an amazing or historical goal can be overturned in a way that it wouldn't without VAR. The biggest moment that could have changed history with VAR would have been Maradona's handball against England which gave Argentina the lead to make the game 1-0. Without the goal which would 100% be overturned by VAR history could have been changed and Argentina may not have became World Champions for the 2nd time. There many other cases big or small like offside goals, mistaken penalties, or even when the goalkeepers dived in pens well after the line and it was allowed because it was difficult to regulate.

Is VAR healthy for Football?

Yea but not in its current state. Currently VAR has too many mistakes that have to be polished with newer technology that can be adapted to all major leagues minimum. A fair game will always be better, but the time between decisions needs to be reduced and reducing the chance of errors. Overall Football refereeing will change as society has changed through technology. The best thing Football can do is adapt for the health of the game. Different doesn't mean it worse or better, it's the way that it's managed that will cause a positive or negative impact.
