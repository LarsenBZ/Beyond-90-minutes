Real Madrid Have Replaced Kroos and Modrić. But Have They Replaced What They Provided?

By Braulio Zelaya · August 2026 · 3 min read

Since 2014/15 Kroos, and Modric along Casemiro became the trio of the 3 consecutive Champions Leagues and their 4th in season 21/22. In the summer of 22/23 Casemiro left but Modric and Kroos showed that they had the level to win again. With the signing of Jude Bellingham, Real Madrid would form a midfield with Kroos, Tchouameni, Valverde and Bellingham in a 4-4-2 formation. Modric was still important as a sub for the team during the 23/24 season. Kroos would be the one to manage the ball in Real Madrid advancing the ball when he needed to and slowing the game when needed. This was the last season of Toni Kroos winning his 6th Champions League along with Modric. This also became the last major title won by Real Madrid as of August 2026.

"There is no player in Real Madrid's current squad that has the playstyle to control the game like Kroos."

Who is at fault for 2 consecutive seasons of poor performances?

Many point fingers at Kylian Mbappe as he is seen as the disrupter of the group as he was signing after Real Madrid won the Champions League. Others point at Vinicius Jr for the same reason highlighting his rumored bad view of Xabi Alonso during the 25/26 season. In that season there was also a leaked fight between Tchouameni and Valverde. Others point fingers at the defensive as players such as Trent Alexander-Arnold, Rudiger, Raul Asencio, and Alvaro Carreras, which didn't show a strong showing against top teams and mid-table La Liga teams.

Are these all of the reasons for the two poor performing seasons?

Yes and No. Yes, it's a mixture of it all but tactically the Real Madrid board has failed to sign players can do the job of Kroos and Modric.

Analyzing the Real Madrid 26/27 lineup

Real Madrid will most likely form a 4-2-3-1 or a 4-3-3 lineup. Formed by the Courtois as the goalkeeper. A defensive line of: Cucurella, Huijsen, Rudiger, TAA/Dumfries. Amidfield composed of: Tchouameni/Valverde, Valverde/Bernardo Silva, and Bellingham as an offensive midfield. An attack formed by Vinicius in left wing, Mbappe as striker with the help of Bellingham, and Yan Diomande/Endrick.

What does this tell us about Real Madrid?

Real Madrid was able to sign 6 players and only 1 of them is a midfielder. Bernardo is an experienced player who could do the job of Modric but, there is no player in Real Madrid's current squad that has the playstyle to control the game like Kroos. The biggest Real Madrid problem is that against big teams Real Madrid can't get the ball or keep the ball for long periods of time. Against mall teams Real Madrid can have the ball, but the players seem to not know how to open the score and makes it difficult for Real Madrid to win games against low block La Liga teams.

Can Real Madrid win with the current squad?

Every single game has to be played out. Real Madrid has the attack to win against big teams in UCL and a squad to be consistent in La Liga, but it will be difficult. The defensive line gained a big upgrade with Cucurella and Dumfries who could leave Trent out of the starting XI. It will be Mourinho's job to bring back Real Madrid to winning.

Rodri to Barcelona

Rodri has signed for Barcelona after being in talks with Real Madrid but in the end Real Madrid lost interest/Rodri got tired of waiting and decided to join Barcelona. Real Madrid signing maybe not Rodri, but a player like Rodri like Kroos in the past would have been extremely helpful for Mourinho as none of his players can do the same at a high level. Only time will tell now if Rodri made the right decision or if in the end Mourinho did not need a deep-lying midfielder.
